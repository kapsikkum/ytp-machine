snes_bank.npz is cut down from ExpressiveSNES.sf2 (dated 06/09/2024) by
DitherEmotion -- Discord: ditheremotion, YouTube:
https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCn-elu9bCClVunLThNpgFUw, Twitter: @ditheremotion.

The font is a General MIDI SoundFont of samples ripped from SNES games, mostly
lesser-known ones. Its author wrote that anyone may do anything with it,
including editing it into new versions, and asks for credit on those versions.
This bank is one: one sample per program and drum key, loops on 16-sample
blocks, built with

    python scripts/build_snes_bank.py ExpressiveSNES.sf2 --store-hz 32000 --ripped

The samples themselves belong to whoever made the games they came from, as the
font's own copyright field says.
