YTPMACHINE
say ytpmv splice corpus
Type a word. You get the sounds it needs, what the splicer currently does with it, and every word in the corpus that could supply each piece.
Write words that already sound right (mick nug), a lone consonant for its sound (zoo p), ARPAbet if you know it, =letters to spell it out, or =skip to never build it.
Click the gaps between sounds to move the cuts. Each piece is then taken from one word, whole or trimmed. Every candidate clip can be heard before you choose it, and each piece shows its clip's waveform: drag the yellow edges to move exactly where it is cut, click anywhere on it to hear from there, space to hear the cut again.

Saving writes the pieces down against this corpus, and every future generation of the word uses them instead of searching. The search is good at most words and blind to the ones it gets wrong, which is what this is for.
