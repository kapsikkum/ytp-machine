how parts work, and what this voice has besides words

Every part has a suggested word — picked for the kind of sound the part makes (a pop for a kick, a hiss for a hi-hat, a long vowel for a tune). Change any of them: type a word or a phrase, try another take of the same word, or click an alternative. ▶ part plays a few seconds of that part on its own. octave moves a part up or down without changing the tune — the suggestion puts each part where the voice sounds natural. instrument picks what plays a part: auto follows the chip, anything else stays put whatever the chip is — another machine's voice, or voice to keep the voice as recorded. When an OPL or the SNES plays a part, patch picks which instrument.
