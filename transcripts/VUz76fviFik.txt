There's an old shop in Islington called "Schram and Scheddle." Today, it's a shop that sells "Preposterous Presents."
I imagine, that a long time ago, it was a tailor shop... and it went like this: Mr. Schram was a boss. Mr. Scheddle sat at the sewing machine, with his foot on the pedal, sewing away.Sometimes, Mr. Schram thought Mr. Scheddle wasn't working hard enough, so he shouted:
"Pedal, Scheddle!" And Scheddle snarled back:
"Scram, Schram!" And so it went on, day after day:
"Pedal, Scheddle!" "Scram, Schram!" "Pedal, Scheddle!" "Scram, Schram!" "Pedal, Scheddle!" "Scram, Schram!"
